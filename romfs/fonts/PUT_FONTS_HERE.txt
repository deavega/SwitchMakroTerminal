Place two TrueType fonts here before building:

  main.ttf   -> regular weight
  bold.ttf   -> bold weight (optional; falls back to main.ttf if missing)

Any TTF works. Good free choices with the "terminal / dashboard" look:
  - Inter        (https://github.com/rsms/inter) -> Inter-Regular.ttf / Inter-Bold.ttf
  - IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Mono
  - JetBrains Mono
  - Roboto / Roboto Condensed

Example (from the project root, after downloading a TTF):
  cp ~/Downloads/Inter-Regular.ttf romfs/fonts/main.ttf
  cp ~/Downloads/Inter-Bold.ttf    romfs/fonts/bold.ttf

The app renders ▲ ▼ • arrows/bullets, so pick a font that includes those
glyphs (Inter and IBM Plex both do). You can change these paths in config.json.
